Ich gestalte bei der Würth International AG in Chur Produktbilder, Labels, Printmedien und digitale Inhalte – von der Kampagnenidee bis zum fertigen Asset, präzise im Corporate Design.

Als gelernter Mediamatiker bringe ich Gestaltung, Technik und Medienproduktion zusammen. At Würth International AG in Chur, I create product imagery, labels, print media and digital content – from campaign idea to finished asset, precisely within the corporate design.

As a trained Mediamatiker (multimedia specialist), I combine design, technology and media production. Alongside my job, I study Digital Business Management at FH Graubünden – I am interested in how good design and digital tools turn into new business models. I also mentor apprentices through their final vocational project (IPA).
